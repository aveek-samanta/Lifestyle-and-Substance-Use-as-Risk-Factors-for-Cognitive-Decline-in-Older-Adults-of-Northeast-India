# Lifestyle-and-Substance-Use-as-Risk-Factors-for-Cognitive-Decline-in-Older-Adults-of-Northeast-India

## 📌 Project Title

**Examining the Risk of Substance Use and Lifestyle Factors on Cognitive Decline Among Older People in North-Eastern States of India**

This repository contains the research materials for a term paper analyzing how **substance use and lifestyle behaviors influence cognitive decline among older adults in North-Eastern India**.

The study focuses on identifying **modifiable behavioral and social risk factors** associated with cognitive impairment among ageing populations. Using nationally representative survey data and statistical modelling, the research provides evidence on how lifestyle behaviors such as **alcohol consumption, sleep disturbance, and social engagement** affect cognitive health.

---

# 🎯 Research Objectives

The main objective of this study is to examine the relationship between **substance use, lifestyle factors, and cognitive impairment among older adults**.

The research addresses the following questions:

* Does **tobacco consumption** influence cognitive impairment among older adults?
* Is **alcohol consumption** associated with higher risk of cognitive decline?
* Which **lifestyle factors** (sleep, reading habits, physical activity, social participation) influence cognitive health?

---

# 📍 Study Area

The research focuses on the **North-Eastern region of India**, including the following states:

* Arunachal Pradesh
* Assam
* Manipur
* Meghalaya
* Mizoram
* Nagaland
* Tripura

This region is particularly important because it has **higher prevalence of tobacco and alcohol consumption compared to many other parts of India**, making it a critical area for studying substance-related health risks.

---

# 📊 Data Source

**Longitudinal Ageing Study in India (LASI), Wave-1 (2017–18)**

Key characteristics:

* Nationally representative survey on ageing in India
* Covers **72,000+ respondents aged 45 and above**
* Analytical sample restricted to **7,790 older adults from North-Eastern states**

The LASI dataset provides detailed information on **health, lifestyle behaviors, socio-economic status, and cognitive functioning** among older adults.

---

# 🧪 Methodology

The study applies quantitative statistical analysis to examine the determinants of cognitive impairment.

### Statistical Techniques

* Descriptive statistics
* Chi-square test of association
* Binary logistic regression

### Outcome Variable

Cognitive impairment measured using a **word recall test (0–10 scale)**

* ≤4 words recalled → High cognitive impairment
* ≥5 words recalled → Low cognitive impairment

### Key Explanatory Variables

* Tobacco consumption (smoked or smokeless)
* Alcohol consumption
* Sleep disturbance
* Reading habits
* Physical activity
* Cultural and social participation
* Food security

### Control Variables

* Age
* Sex
* Education
* Residence (rural/urban)
* Marital status
* Caste
* Living arrangement
* Wealth index

All statistical analyses were conducted using **STATA 17**.

---

# 🔎 Key Findings

The study highlights several important determinants of cognitive impairment:

* **Advancing age** is the strongest predictor of cognitive decline.
* **Alcohol consumption** significantly increases the risk of cognitive impairment.
* **Sleep disturbances** show a strong dose-response relationship with cognitive decline.
* **Marital disruption** and **food insecurity** increase vulnerability to cognitive impairment.
* **Tobacco use becomes statistically insignificant after adjustment**, suggesting confounding effects.

These findings emphasize the importance of **modifiable lifestyle behaviors in maintaining cognitive health among older adults**.

---

# 📈 Policy Implications

The results suggest several public health interventions:

* Early **cognitive screening programs** for older adults
* Community-level **alcohol reduction initiatives**
* Awareness campaigns promoting **healthy sleep practices**
* Encouraging **social engagement and cultural participation**
* Addressing **food insecurity among elderly populations**

These measures can support **healthy ageing and cognitive well-being** in North-Eastern India.
